Age: Is only an approximate indicator.  There a is very small chance that a character could die before that age, but as one approaches the age listed, the chance of dying increases.

Exp: The higher the number, the more experience is needed to make a level.

Stats & Alignments: The numbers under the stats are in a low/high format.  For example: Str 7/17 means that a character of that race cannot start with a Strength of less than 7, nor greater than 17.  You may not raise a NATURAL stat more than 5 above the maximum listed above.  G,N and E represent the alignments the specified races can be.
