Resistant Spells

 

Initially created by the Sorcerers guild to protect themselves from their own destructive forces, these spells are very useful in increasing the resistance of a character from many forms of attack.  The Healers guild also helped in this study and created spells to increase ones resistance to Poison and Disease.

 

Resist Fire  

Base Level: 3 Guilds: See (9), Sor, Wiz (9), Hea (6)

Required Stats: 13 Int, 13 Wis, 13 Cha  

NON-COMBAT Spell, will raise the recipient's Fire Resistance to 95%.
