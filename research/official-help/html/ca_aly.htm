Banish Spells

 

Taken from the art of demonology, spells of this kind are of ancient magic, of which little is known.  Used to banish demons and devils back to the planes of Hell from whence they came, the amount of damage inflicted by these spells can sometimes be extensive.  The effectiveness of these spells depends on the caster's experience and the Magic Resistance of the monster.

 

Banish Devil  

Base Level: 5 Guilds: Wiz (10), Hea

Required Stats: 13 Int, 15 Wis, 10 Con  

COMBAT Spell, will cause 8 Monsters in 4 Groups to be Banished.  

An ancient spell that invokes the fury of Hell to yank the specified target(s) back to the plane from whence they came. 

 

Banish Demon  

Base Level: 6 Guilds: Wiz (12), Hea

Required Stats: 12 Int, 14 Wis, 10 Con  

COMBAT Spell, will cause 8 Monsters in 4 Groups to be Banished.  

A dark spell of old, much like Banish Devil, this spell calls upon Hades to take back those who have escaped.
