Magical Spells

 

Spells of this nature vary depending on type.  Being purely magical and created by various guilds, spells of this type usually revolve around some effect that is not supported by any of the other classes.

 

Charm of Opening  

Base Level: 1 Guilds: Nom (12), Nin (9), Thi(3), Sca (6)

Required Stats: 12 Str, 13 Int, 13 Wis, 13 Dex  

NON-COMBAT Spell, will open Magically Locked chests.

Casting this spell will unlock a magically locked chest.
